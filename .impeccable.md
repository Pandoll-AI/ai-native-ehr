## Design Context

### Users
- **Primary**: Healthcare investors, medical partners, clinical leadership evaluating AI EHR/EHR solutions
- **Secondary**: Practicing clinicians (physicians, nurses) curious about next-gen EHR workflows
- **Context**: Viewing on desktop/mobile during evaluation or after hearing about AI Native EHR. Decision-makers who see dozens of health-tech pitches. Need to quickly understand "why this is different."
- **Job to be done**: Assess whether this team truly understands both medicine AND AI, and whether this product is worth early engagement.

### Brand Personality
- **Bold** — Makes definitive claims, not hedged promises. "Rebuilt from Scratch."
- **Future-forward** — Positions ahead of the industry, not catching up.
- **Clear** — Complex clinical AI concepts made instantly understandable.

### Emotional Goals
- **Confidence**: "This team understands medicine AND AI. This isn't another ChatGPT wrapper."
- **Relief**: "Someone is finally rebuilding EHR from the ground up."
- Trust through demonstrated depth, not through buzzwords.

### Anti-references (MUST NOT look like)
- ❌ Legacy EHR/EHR software (Epic, Cerner — dated enterprise UI, blue gradients, stock photos of doctors)
- ❌ Over-the-top tech/gaming aesthetic (neon, cyberpunk, hacker terminal)
- ❌ Generic AI startup landing pages (purple gradients, ChatGPT-wrapper vibes, "powered by AI" badges)

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Template**: Glassmorphism Card from design-prompts-gallery
- **Theme**: Dark immersive hero transitioning to clean light sections
- **Palette**: Pure black (#000), zinc (#18181B), light (#F4F4F5), emerald highlight (#34D399)
- **Typography**: Inter — single family, weights 300-700. Headings tight (-0.05em), labels bold uppercase 10px
- **Glass effects**: rgba(255,255,255,0.05) + blur(12px) + white/10 border
- **Radius**: 2.5rem containers, 1-1.5rem internal cards
- **Grain**: 15% opacity noise SVG on dark sections
- **Motion**: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), 0.8s entry, 105% hover scale

### Design Principles

1. **Depth over decoration** — Use glassmorphism, layering, and lighting (emerald glow, grain texture) to create visual depth. Never add ornament for ornament's sake.

2. **Dark = immersive, Light = functional** — Dark sections (hero, how-it-works, CTA, footer) create emotional impact. Light sections (features, timeline) deliver information clearly. The contrast between them creates rhythm.

3. **Clinical precision, not clinical coldness** — Typography is tight and confident. Emerald is the single warm accent that signals "alive" and "active" against monochrome. Avoid sterile blue.

4. **Show the system, not the screen** — Feature mockups are shown inside device frames, but the real story is the ontology engine, knowledge graph, and AI agents. Code snippets and system analysis cards reveal the architecture, not just the UI.

5. **Earned trust through specificity** — Use real ICD codes, real SOAP note structures, real clinical data patterns in mockups. Generic placeholder text ("Lorem ipsum") is forbidden. Every data point should feel like it came from a real clinical encounter.
